## What it does

Invoice Match AI automatically reconciles incoming supplier invoices against purchase orders and goods-receipt notes, flags duplicate billings, and routes exceptions for approval — cutting manual AP workload by up to 80%.

## Key features

- **3-way matching** — invoice ↔ PO ↔ receipt matching with tolerance rules per vendor.
- **Duplicate-billing radar** — fuzzy detection of double submissions across vendors, dates, and amounts.
- **Exception inbox** — only mismatches reach a human; clean invoices auto-post.
- **Vendor scorecards** — error rates and pricing drift per supplier over time.
- **ERP-ready exports** — CSV/API handoff to your accounting stack.

## Who it's for

Finance and accounts-payable teams in manufacturing, logistics, and field services processing 500+ invoices/month.
