# Use Cases — Invoice Match AI

[Live app](https://ziontechgroup.com/invoice-match-ai/) · [Plans](https://ziontechgroup.com/en/plans/) · [Discovery call](https://ziontechgroup.com/discovery/)

## 1. Manufacturing AP automation
A precision manufacturer processing 2,000 invoices/month auto-matches 85% of invoices to POs and receipts; only exceptions reach the AP clerk, freeing 1.5 FTE.

## 2. Duplicate-billing recovery
A logistics firm catches 37 duplicate invoices in the first quarter — €41,000 recovered from double submissions by two carriers.

## 3. Multi-entity shared services
A group with 6 subsidiaries centralizes AP; tolerance rules per entity and currency keep matching accurate across borders.

## 4. Vendor drift detection
Price creep on a top supplier is flagged when unit prices diverge from PO terms by >3%, triggering a renegotiation via [Contract Renewal Radar](https://ziontechgroup.com/contract-renewal-radar/).

## 5. Fuel-card reconciliation
Fleet fuel invoices are matched automatically against telematics from [Fleet Fuel Optimizer](https://ziontechgroup.com/fleet-fuel-optimizer/) data.
